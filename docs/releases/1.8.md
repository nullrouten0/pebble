# Pebble 1.8 — take to the sky

Pebble 1.8 adds the first creatures you can fly: a **pegasus** and a
**fire-breathing dragon**. It also rolls up everything from the 1.7 releases:
the **Creative Tools menu**, real icons for the building tools, and a handful
of fixes. Your worlds carry over as they are.

## Flying mounts

**Ride a pegasus or a dragon through the sky.** Saddle one, right-click it to climb
on (tap it on iPad and iPhone) and fly it the way creative flight works:

| Key | While riding |
|---|---|
| W | Fly where you're looking |
| Space | Take off and climb |
| Shift | Descend; on the ground, get off |
| Sprint | Fly faster |
| Attack (hold) | Dragon only: breathe fire |

On the ground they walk like a horse. On iPad and iPhone, tap one to climb on,
and the jump and sneak buttons climb and descend. Landing never hurts you or
your mount.

**Getting one.**
- **Placed ones come ready to ride**, tamed and saddled. Use the top of the
  Creatures tab in the Creative Tools menu, a spawn egg, `/summon pegasus`
  or `/spawn dragon`.
- **Wild ones roam the world.** Pegasi live in meadows and cherry groves, and
  dragons on mountain peaks. They take short flights of their own and fly
  off when you hit them.
- **Taming a wild one** takes food: golden carrots or golden apples for a
  pegasus, and raw meat or fish for a dragon. Each feeding has a one-in-three
  chance; hearts mean it worked. Then put a saddle on it. Click a wild or
  unsaddled one and it tells you what it needs.

**The dragon breathes fire.** Hold attack while riding to set everything
living in a cone ahead of you alight, up to 10 blocks away. You and your
tamed pets are spared, and it never burns blocks, so your builds are safe.
Fire and lava can't hurt a dragon.

**LAN guests can ride, fly and breathe fire** too.

## Riding, for every mount

- **Shift gets you off.** Until now there was no way to get off a horse,
  pig, strider or camel yourself. A fresh Shift press steps you off beside
  your mount. On a flying mount Shift descends instead, so land, let go and
  press it again.
- **The camera glides with your mount** instead of jumping along 20 times a
  second. In third person (F5) it sits further back on a big mount, so the
  dragon's head and wings stay in view.

## The Creative Tools menu (1.7)

Everything the building and demolition releases added, without typing. Press
**G** (rebindable in Options → Controls), or tap the toolbox button at the top
of a touch screen. It's for creative mode only.

- **Tools** puts a tool in your hand: the bazooka, mining laser, void bomb,
  quarry charge, wall and box builders, Infinity Wipe, selection wand and
  headlamp.
- **Structures** places any `/place` structure in front of you, with a
  choice of layout.
- **Creatures** spawns any creature in front of you, or all ten anime animals
  at once.
- **Edit** has undo, redo, selection corners, copy, cut and paste, which puts
  undo and redo one tap away on iPad and iPhone.

The menu stays open, so you can place, spawn and undo in a row. Hovering an
entry shows the command it runs, with the tooltip below the entry so it
doesn't cover it. Guests in a LAN game can use it too.

`/spawn <creature>` also brings a single creature in front of you, on the
ground and facing you.

## Also new

- **The seven building and demolition tools have real icons**, in place of
  the coloured blobs they've had since 1.1. A thrown void bomb now looks like
  one in flight.
- **Particles that never showed now do.** Since 1.0, flames, smoke, hearts,
  crits, rain and snow splashes, petals and music notes drew only an empty
  corner of their picture, so you couldn't see them. Expect the world to look
  a little livelier.

## Fixes

- **On iPad and iPhone, tapping a creature uses your hand on it** when that
  does something: climb on a saddled mount, put on a saddle, feed an animal,
  board a boat or trade with a villager. Otherwise the tap hits it, and
  press-and-hold still attacks. A tap used to always attack, so touch screens
  had no way to ride, saddle or trade.
- **Spawning lots of creatures with the Blight among them** could crash the
  game. Once it finished charging and went after a creature directly below it,
  the zero distance between them sent it flying off to nowhere. It now just
  hovers. An XP orb exactly at your eye had the same problem and is fixed too.
- **A safety net:** a creature asked to move by an impossible amount, from
  any bug like this, now stands still instead of crashing the game.

## Upgrading

- **Mac:** `git pull && ./pebble install`.
- **iPad and iPhone:** pull, run `./pebble ios`, and press ⌘R in Xcode.
- **LAN games need everyone on the same version**, so update every device.

925 regression checks pass. [CHANGELOG.md](../../CHANGELOG.md) has every
change in full (1.7.0 to 1.8.1).
