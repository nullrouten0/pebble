# Pebble 1.12 — the floor builder, and copy and paste for LAN guests

A new building tool that pours floors like paint, and everyone in a LAN game
can now copy, cut, paste and use blueprints, not just the host.

## The floor builder (1.12.1)

Get it with `/give floor_builder` or from the Creative Tools menu, hold a
block in your off hand (or in the hotbar slot right of it), and right-click.

It works like a paint can. The floor starts where a block would go if you
placed one, then spreads level from there through every open space it can
reach: across the room, through doorways, around pillars and furniture, and
behind things as long as there's a way round. Walls stop it, and it never
replaces a block that's already there, not even a torch. Two blocks that only
touch at their corners still stop it.

- Click the ground inside a room to lay a floor one block up.
- Click the side of a wall to lay a ceiling, or the next storey, at that
  height.
- In the open it stops 16 blocks out each way (8 if you sneak).
- One `//undo` (Cmd+Z) takes the whole floor back.

## Copy and paste together

A guest marks a box the same way the host does: the selection wand (left
click for one corner, right click for the other) or `//pos1` and `//pos2`.
Then `//copy`, `//cut` and `//paste` work as they always have, and so do the
Copy, Cut and Paste entries on the Creative Tools menu's Edit tab.

Each player has their own selection and their own clipboard, so two people
can copy different things at once. `//undo` (Cmd+Z) takes back only your own
paste or cut.

## Your blueprints, in someone else's world

A guest's blueprints are their own:

- **`//save castle`** saves what's on your clipboard as a blueprint on your
  own device, even while you're in someone else's world.
- **`//load castle`** loads one of your blueprints onto your clipboard, ready
  to `//paste` into the host's world.
- **`//blueprints`** and the Edit tab's **Load …** entries list yours.

So you can bring a build from your own world to a friend's game, or take
something you built together home with you.

## Good to know

- Both players need Pebble 1.12. With an older host, copy and paste find
  nothing to copy, and `//load` doesn't answer.
- Your clipboard lasts while you're in the game. Like the host's, it's empty
  again after you leave and rejoin.
