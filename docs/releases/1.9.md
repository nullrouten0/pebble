# Pebble 1.9 — the alicorn and the griffin

Two more flying mounts join the pegasus and the dragon, each with a power
you use by holding attack while you ride.

## The alicorn

A winged unicorn: a lilac coat, a purple mane and tail, and a gold spiral
horn. **Its horn casts magic bolts**, about three a second, at whatever your
crosshair is on, up to 32 blocks away. Each bolt hurts the first creature it
meets and stops at the first block, so it never breaks anything.

Wild alicorns live in **flower forests** and **sunflower plains**. Tame one
with **golden apples** or **glistering melon slices**.

## The griffin

An eagle's white head, hooked gold beak, brown wings and yellow talons on a
lion's body. **It's the fastest flyer.** Hold attack and **it screeches**:
every creature within 10 blocks is thrown back and runs from it for six
seconds, and a monster forgets it was after you. Handy for clearing a
landing spot.

Wild griffins live in **windswept hills** and on **savanna plateaus**. Tame
one with **raw meat**.

## For both

- Neither power touches you or your tamed pets. The screech also spares
  other players and the bosses (Warden, Rift Dragon, Blight).
- A spawn egg, `/summon alicorn`, `/spawn griffin` or the top of the Creative
  Tools menu's Creatures tab brings one tamed and saddled, ready to ride.
- Riding works like every flying mount: W flies where you look, Space climbs,
  Shift descends, sprint goes faster. On iPad and iPhone, tap to climb on and
  press-and-hold to use the power.
- LAN guests can ride them and use their powers too.
