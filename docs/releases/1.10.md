# Pebble 1.10 — share a world

Send a world to a friend, or to your own iPad or other Mac, in a couple of
taps.

## Sharing a world

In **Singleplayer**, select a world and click **Share**. The system share
sheet opens:

- **AirDrop** to a nearby Mac, iPad or iPhone.
- **Messages** or **Mail** to anyone.
- **Save to File…** on the Mac, or **Save to Files** on iPad and iPhone, for
  a backup or to move it yourself.

The world goes as a single `.pebbleworld` file with everything saved for it:
the land you've explored and changed, your builds, chests and signs, your
inventory and where you were standing, and your advancements.

## Opening one

Open the file on the device it arrives on:

- **Mac:** double-click it in Finder.
- **iPad and iPhone:** accept it from AirDrop, or tap it in Messages or Files
  and choose Pebble.
- **Or** click **Import** in Singleplayer and pick the file.

It's added to your worlds, selected and ready to play. It's always a copy:
nothing you already have is replaced, and a world whose name you already
have comes in as "Name (2)".

Both devices need Pebble 1.10 or later. Use the same version where you can:
when an older Pebble opens a world from a newer one, blocks and items it
doesn't have yet are dropped.
