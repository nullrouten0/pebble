# Pebble 1.11 — blueprints

Build something once, then use it in any world, as often as you like, or
send it to a friend.

## Saving and loading

1. Mark the build with the selection wand (or `//pos1` and `//pos2`).
2. `//copy` it, then **`//save castle`**. (If you haven't copied anything,
   `//save` takes your selection.)
3. In any world, **`//load castle`** puts it on your clipboard. Look where
   you want it and **`//paste`**, as many times as you like.

Chests keep their contents and signs keep their text. **`//blueprints`** lists
what you've saved, and `//blueprints delete castle` removes one. The Creative
Tools menu's Edit tab lists every blueprint as a **Load …** entry, so on
iPad and iPhone it's a tap.

## Sharing

**`//blueprints share castle`** opens the share sheet: AirDrop, Messages,
Mail or Save to File. When your friend opens the `.pebbleblueprint` file,
Pebble adds it to their blueprints, ready to `//load`.

## Built to last

A blueprint stores what each block and item is called, not its number, the
same way worlds do. One you save today still loads after future updates add
or reorder blocks. Anything a version doesn't have is left out, and `//load`
tells you what.

In a LAN game, only the host can save and load blueprints for now, as with
copy and paste.
